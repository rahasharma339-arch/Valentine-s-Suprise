# 💕 Valentine Surprise Website

A surprise Valentine's website with games, timers, and a sweet message. **Frontend only** — no backend needed.

## How to run

1. Open the folder on your computer.
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to open it in your browser.

   **Or** from the project folder run a simple server (optional):
   - **Node (npx):** `npx serve` then open the URL it shows

## What’s inside

- **Name screen** — Enter your name and your partner’s name, then start.
- **Valentine prompt** — “Will you be my Valentine?” with **Yes** and **No**. The No button runs away across the screen when you try to click it. After you choose Yes, you see “Okay, let’s begin!” and go to the games.
- **Game hub** — “Valentine’s surprise for [name]” with 6 games. Animated heart collectors move along the bottom and collect falling hearts; catching hearts can award small bonus points.
- **Games (all timed, points add up):**
  - **Heart Maze** — Reach the 💘 in 45 seconds. Use on-screen arrows or keyboard (WASD / Arrow keys). **80 points** when you reach the goal.
  - **Finance + Love Quiz** — 10 questions, **10 seconds per question**. **10 points** per correct answer (finance + love themed, with hidden messages).
  - **Heart Slide Puzzle** — Solve the 3×3 sliding puzzle before **90 seconds**. **90 points** on completion.
  - **Word Scramble** — Unscramble 5 words before **60 seconds**. **60 points** when all are correct.
  - **Money Math Sprint** — **60 seconds** of finance-style math. **10 points** per correct answer (max 100).
  - **Catch the Hearts** — Move the basket with arrows or on-screen buttons. Catch 💗 hearts (**+10** each), avoid 💣 bombs (3 lives). **60 seconds**.
- **Win condition** — Get **300+ points** to enable **“Claim my surprise!”**.
- **Surprise screen** — Cartoon figures hugging + “Happy Valentine’s Day!” + confetti. Then **“Read your message”**.
- **Message screen** — The love message is shown **jumbled** (scrambled words). Click **“Reveal the message”** to unjumble it **word by word** (typing-style reveal). **“Play again”** returns to the name screen.

Enjoy the surprise.
